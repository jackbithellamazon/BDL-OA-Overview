Stock Health Targets
0–90d flow
The Stock Health page measures what share of stock sells within 90 days. The goal is what you always aim for; the floor is your realistic minimum — fall below it and the page flags it red. Used across the hero gauge, the trend graph bands and recent entries.
Provider Order
—
Define your providers and drag to set the order they appear in across the Provider page, charts, and the snapshot form. New providers you type into a snapshot are added here automatically — reorder them any time. Removing a provider here only takes it off the ordered list; its history stays until you delete it from the provider's detail popup.
SKU Matching Rules
—
Used by the SKU importer in Add Snapshot. A SKU matches a provider if any underscore-separated segment equals one of its aliases (exact match, trailing digits ignored — TFTJ2 → TFTJ), or starts with one of its stems (prefix match — tftj matches TFTJH, TFTJ5…). Title keywords only kick in as a last resort, when the SKU itself matched nothing. Assigning an unresolved code to a provider during import adds it here automatically as a new alias.
Archived Providers
0 archived
Archived providers are hidden from the Provider page, charts, KPIs and the snapshot form — but every snapshot is kept and still appears under History → Provider Snapshots. Restore one any time to resume tracking. Logging a new snapshot for an archived provider also restores it automatically.
